# STEP RULES: FOLLOW-UP MAIL (sequence_step 2 or more)

This mail follows an earlier mail to the same lead. previous_angles lists the angles already used, in order.

- Length: under 60 words.
- Each follow-up must use an angle not listed in previous_angles. Never repeat the pitch. Never say "following up", "as I said" or "per my last mail".
- Never ask a yes or no question. Ask for a reply with a choice, a small task, or a plain statement.
- Subject: for steps 2 and 3, leave subject empty (the mail goes in the same thread). For step 4 or more, write 2 to 5 words, all lowercase.
- Links: step 2 has no link. Step 3 may have a link only if SENDER proof_or_none contains one. Step 4 or more has no link.
- End with the sender first name on its own line.

## Step 2 (about 3 days later)
- Job: add one new, useful fact or angle from SENDER or LEAD. Do not repeat the first mail.
- Call to action: offer a small, easy next step (SENDER low_friction_offer if it is not empty).

## Step 3 (about 7 days later)
- Job: show proof. Use SENDER proof_or_none only if it is not "none". If it is "none", use a short, honest observation about the lead's problem instead.
- Call to action: ask which of two options fits the lead better.

## Step 4 or more (about 12 days later or more)
- Job: close the loop. Say plainly that this is the last mail on this topic. Leave the door open. No pressure.
- Call to action: tell the lead to reply with one word if they want it later.
